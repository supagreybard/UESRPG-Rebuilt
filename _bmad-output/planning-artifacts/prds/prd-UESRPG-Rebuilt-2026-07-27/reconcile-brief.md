# Reconciliation: Product Brief To PRD

Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/briefs/brief-UESRPG-Rebuilt-2026-07-27/brief.md`

## Verdict

The PRD captures the product brief's core intent and incorporates the follow-up decisions from the PRD setup conversation.

## Captured From Brief

- UESRPG Rebuilt is a Foundry VTT system for UESRPG 3e v4.
- 1.0 goal is complete play at the table, not only build/package success.
- Primary users are GMs and players.
- Core mechanics include skill tests, characteristic tests, opposed tests, and combat support.
- Play documents include character sheets, statblocks, items, equipment, and magic-relevant sheets.
- Magic scope includes spellcasting, ritual spellcasting, alchemy, and mishaps.
- Guided workflows include character creation, advancement, spell creation, enchanting, alchemy, equipment builders, and statblock creation.
- GM tools include XP reward support, treasure tables, and artifact generation.
- Compendium content is treated as a product feature, subject to rights and provenance constraints.
- Foundry-native behavior, localization, package alignment, and runtime validation are explicit constraints.

## Captured From PRD Setup Conversation

- The PRD is a solo/internal planning artifact.
- 1.0 is aimed at other UESRPG groups installing and running with minimal handholding.
- Greybard is the ruleset source until a formal rules coverage checklist exists.
- Wizards/builders are required as content-authoring tools, not merely optional UX polish.

## Remaining Gaps

- Exact rules coverage checklist for fields, rolls, formulas, and edge cases.
- Minimum compendium coverage by entity type and count.
- Provenance tracking format for release-ready System Content.
- Representative external-user or simulated minimal-handholding validation scenario.
